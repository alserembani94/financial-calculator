# Malaysia Budget 2027: announced measures for resident individual income tax (YA2027)

All sources accessed 6 November 2026 unless stated otherwise. Status: **every item below is a proposal**. The Finance Bill that would amend the Income Tax Act 1967 to give effect to these measures had not been found as tabled or passed as of 6 Nov 2026 (see Q6).

Primary sources used (the official MOF PDFs were downloaded from belanjawan.mof.gov.my and their text extracted locally; quotes below are from that extracted text, in Malay, with my English gloss):
- **Ucapan Belanjawan 2027** (speech text, Malay; the printed edition, "diterbitkan pada 15 Oktober 2026"), which also reproduces the tax measures as "Lampiran II – Langkah Cukai Belanjawan 2027" — [MOF ub27.pdf](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf)
- **Langkah Cukai Belanjawan 2027** (tax measures, 36 appendices, Malay) — [MOF langkah-cukai.pdf](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/langkah-cukai.pdf)

## Q1. Speech date, what the PM said on rates, and the full proposed resident bracket table vs YA2026

### Takeaway
Budget 2027 was tabled on Friday 9 October 2026 by PM and Finance Minister Anwar Ibrahim. The official tax-measures appendix (Lampiran 1) cuts 70,001–100,000 from 19% to 18%, splits off a new 100,001–150,000 band at 24% (was 25%), keeps 150,001–400,000 at 25%, shortens the 28% band to 600,001–1,000,000, and applies 30% to income above RM1,000,000 (was above RM2,000,000). It takes effect from YA2027.

### Cited Findings
- **Date:** the speech was delivered in the Dewan Rakyat "pada Jumaat, 9 Oktober 2026 bersamaan 27 Rabiulakhir 1448 Hijrah" — [MOF Ucapan Belanjawan 2027](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf). It was Anwar's fifth MADANI budget ("Ucapan Belanjawan MADANI Kelima") — same source. Tabling time was moved to 3:30pm from 4pm, per a Malay Mail headline only (lower confidence) — [Malay Mail, 6 Oct 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/10/06/2027-budget-to-be-tabled-at-330pm-this-friday-brought-forward-from-4pm/237889)
- **Speech paragraph 29 (verbatim, Malay):** "Kerajaan mendengar keresahan golongan pertengahan (M40) yang cekal bersabar meskipun turut terhimpit beban sara hidup. Maka, sukacita diumumkan: Pertama: Had pelepasan cukai individu ke atas individu dan saudara tanggungan yang kali terakhir disemak pada 2010, dinaikkan daripada 9,000 ringgit kepada 12,000 ringgit. Kedua: Kadar cukai pendapatan individu pemastautin diturunkan sebanyak satu mata peratusan. ● Banjaran pendapatan bercukai antara 70,001 ringgit hingga 100 ribu ringgit diturunkan kepada 18 peratus. ● Banjaran pendapatan bercukai antara 100,001 ringgit hingga 150 ribu ringgit diturunkan kepada 24 peratus." — [MOF Ucapan Belanjawan 2027](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf)
- **Speech paragraph 30 (verbatim):** "Pada masa yang sama, Kerajaan mengambil pendekatan progresif. Kadar cukai bagi golongan yang berpendapatan tinggi iaitu melebihi 1 juta ringgit diselaraskan kepada 30 peratus." — same source
- **Speech paragraph 31 (verbatim):** "Ini bermakna kenaikan pelepasan cukai dan pengurangan kadar cukai individu, bakal memberi lebihan pendapatan boleh guna sehingga 1,600 ringgit kepada kira-kira 5 juta pembayar cukai." (up to RM1,600 extra disposable income for about 5 million taxpayers) — same source

- **Official proposal wording (Lampiran 1, "Cadangan"):** "i. Penurunan 1 mata peratusan bagi banjaran pendapatan bercukai RM70,001 hingga RM100,000 daripada 19% kepada 18%; ii. Mewujudkan banjaran pendapatan bercukai baharu iaitu RM100,001 hingga RM150,000 dengan kadar penurunan 1 mata peratusan daripada 25% kepada 24%. Banjaran pendapatan bercukai RM150,001 hingga RM400,000 kekal 25%; iii. Meminda banjaran pendapatan bercukai RM600,001 hingga RM2,000,000 kepada RM600,001 hingga RM1,000,000 dengan kadar cukai kekal 28%; dan iv. The stated rationale is "Bagi mengimbangi tekanan ekonomi kepada golongan pertengahan" — [MOF Ucapan Belanjawan 2027, Lampiran II](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf); [MOF Langkah Cukai](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/langkah-cukai.pdf)

- A commentary says taxpayers with chargeable income above about RM1.085 million are likely to pay more, not less (search-snippet level, lower confidence) — [Medium, Kin How Chan](https://medium.com/@kinhowchan/malaysia-budget-2027-key-tax-changes-51981b0518a4)

### Inferences
- My own calculation from the official tables (rates only, same chargeable income): the cut saves at most RM800/yr (RM300 on 70k–100k + RM500 on 100k–150k), reached at chargeable income ≥ RM150,000. Above RM1,000,000 the extra 2 points cost up to RM20,000 (at ≥ RM2m), so net rate-only effect is −RM19,200 for chargeable income ≥ RM2m. The rate-only break-even is RM1,040,000.
- Combining the RM3,000 relief increase with the new rates (old CI = X, new CI = X − 3,000): the saving peaks at about RM1,640 (old CI roughly RM603k–RM1m), consistent with the speech's "sehingga 1,600 ringgit". The combined break-even is about RM1,085,500 old CI, matching the Medium commentary's ~RM1.085m. Examples (old CI → saving): RM50k → RM180; RM80k → RM640; RM100k → RM840; RM150k → RM1,520; RM200k → RM1,550; RM1.1m → −RM300; RM2m+ → −RM18,300. These ignore rebates. Check: at RM50k the RM400 rebate is not involved; at incomes near RM35k–38k the relief rise can bring CI under RM35,000 and add the RM400 rebate.
- For a salaried calculator, the bands change only at RM70,001 and above, so most of the YA2027 effect for typical earners comes from the RM12,000 personal relief.

### Gaps
- No English-language official speech text was found on the budget portal (only the Malay "ub27.pdf" was listed). English wording quoted by media is paraphrase.
- The MOF Fiscal Outlook (web page) was not reviewed for the revenue cost of the individual measures. One secondary summary says the measures cost "circa RM3 billion", but this was only a search snippet and it is unclear whether it means the individual measures alone. Unverified.

## Q2. Personal relief (RM9,000 → RM12,000) and other reliefs changed, added or removed

### Takeaway
Self/dependent-relatives relief rises from RM9,000 to RM12,000 (first change since YA2010). The caps on medical (RM10,000), parents (RM8,000), education (RM7,000), lifestyle (RM2,500), sports (RM1,000) and SOCSO/EIS (RM350) are all kept. Their scopes are widened or regrouped, one new additional SOCSO cap of RM150 is added, and tourism relief is extended for YA2027 only. No change was announced to spouse, child, EPF/life insurance, childcare, disabled, SSPN, PRS or other reliefs: they do not appear among the 36 tax-measure appendices.

### Cited Findings
- **Personal relief (Lampiran 2):** current "Mulai tahun taksiran 2010, pelepasan cukai pendapatan individu ke atas individu dan saudara tanggungan dinaikkan daripada RM8,000 kepada RM9,000." Proposal "dinaikkan daripada RM9,000 kepada RM12,000", rationale "Bagi meningkatkan pendapatan boleh guna rakyat dalam mendepani kenaikan kos sara hidup". Effective "Mulai tahun taksiran 2027" — [MOF Langkah Cukai](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/langkah-cukai.pdf)
- **Parents and grandparents (Lampiran 3), cap RM8,000 unchanged:** current scope (from YA2025) is medical treatment, home/daycare/residential care, dental (excluding cosmetic), and full check-ups and vaccination up to RM1,000, supported by a Malaysian Medical Council-registered practitioner certifying that care is needed. Proposal: care at home, daycare and residential care centres widened "untuk meliputi semua jenis perbelanjaan penjagaan tidak terhad kepada penjagaan kesihatan". Claims are to be proven by a written declaration from the carer (not self, spouse or child) or a copy of the carer's work permit with receipts, "tanpa memerlukan pengesahan oleh pengamal perubatan yang berdaftar". From YA2027 — same source
- **Medical → "Kesejahteraan Kesihatan" (Lampiran 4):** the relief is renamed, with the cap "dikekalkan pada RM10,000". Current sub-items: serious disease; fertility; vaccination ≤RM1,000; dental ≤RM1,000; full check-up, mental health and disease screening ≤RM1,000; learning-disability assessment and intervention for a child aged ≤18. Additions: "(i) Perkhidmatan penjagaan selepas bersalin yang disediakan oleh pusat penjagaan atau mana-mana individu yang layak terhad sehingga RM3,000" (postnatal care up to RM3,000); "(ii) Peralatan penyusuan anak berumur 2 tahun dan ke bawah sama ada dibayar oleh diri sendiri atau pasangan terhad sehingga RM1,000". The **separate breastfeeding-equipment relief (RM1,000, own use) is merged into** Kesejahteraan Kesihatan, so it now sits inside the RM10,000 cap instead of standing alone. From YA2027 — same source
- **Education → "Yuran Pengajian dan Latihan Kemahiran" (Lampiran 5), cap kept at RM7,000:** the current relief covers selected fields below Master's and any field for Master's/PhD, plus upskilling courses recognised by the DG of Skills Development up to RM2,000. Lifestyle (RM2,500) separately covers non-work skill courses (hobby, language, religion). Proposal: (i) self, any field, Certificate up to PhD, recognised by any regulator; (ii) self or spouse TVET upskilling courses up to RM3,000; (iii) up to RM2,000 for (a) non-work courses and classes for self or spouse, or (b) STEM, arts, language and religion courses, classes or tuition **for children**. The lifestyle course item is merged into this relief. From YA2027 — same source
- **Lifestyle (Lampiran 6), cap "kekal sehingga RM2,500":** current (from YA2024) covers reading materials, PC, smartphone or tablet, and internet. Added: AI software and app subscriptions for self, spouse or child; pet adoption from a registered animal shelter; registered vet services for vaccination and neutering. From YA2027 — same source
- **Sports (Lampiran 7), cap "kekal sehingga RM1,000":** adds "pembelian kasut sukan sehingga RM300" (sports shoes up to RM300). From YA2027 — same source
- **Tourism (Lampiran 8):** RM1,000 relief on entrance fees to tourist attractions and cultural programmes is "dilanjutkan bagi tempoh setahun" for Visit Malaysia Year 2027. Effective "Bagi tahun taksiran 2027" (YA2027 only) — same source
- **SOCSO/EIS (Lampiran 9):** current RM350 relief (from YA2022) covers private-sector employees' SOCSO and EIS contributions. Proposal: (i) scope extended to mandatory LINDUNG KENDIRI (self-employment) contributions in 3 mandatory sectors (passenger transport, goods/food transport, hawkers), with "had pelepasan sedia ada kekal RM350"; (ii) "Had pelepasan cukai tambahan diberikan sehingga RM150 bagi caruman sukarela pekerja Skim Kemalangan Bukan Bencana Kerja (LINDUNG 24) dan 17 sektor bukan mandatori bagi LINDUNG KENDIRI". From YA2027 — same source. The speech wording is "Pelepasan tambahan diberikan sehingga 150 ringgit bagi caruman sukarela Skim LINDUNG 24 Jam dan LINDUNG Kendiri" — [MOF Ucapan](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf)
- The speech summarises the relief scope changes in para 29 ("Ketiga: Pelepasan cukai individu turut diperluas"): postnatal care, all parent/grandparent care costs, sports shoes, all fields of study and children's tuition, and AI subscriptions, pet vaccination and adoption — [MOF Ucapan](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf)
- The full appendix list (Lampiran 1–36) contains no appendix on spouse, child, EPF/life insurance, PRS, SSPN, childcare, disabled, EV-charging or housing-loan-interest reliefs — [MOF Langkah Cukai, Senarai Lampiran](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/langkah-cukai.pdf)
- Media summaries match the official items: [Malay Mail, 9 Oct 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2026/10/09/budget-2027-individual-tax-relief-rises-to-rm12000-income-tax-percentage-lowered/238401); [YYC Advisors](https://www.yycadvisors.com/budget-2027-malaysia-individual-tax-relief.html) (which says it relies on "LHDN Budget 2027 tax seminar slides")

### Inferences
- Under the merged lifestyle item, the RM2,500 lifestyle cap no longer covers hobby, language or religion courses. They move under the RM7,000 education cap with a RM2,000 sub-limit, which also covers children's tuition. A calculator should model the new sub-limits (TVET RM3,000; non-work and children's STEM etc. RM2,000) inside RM7,000.
- Breastfeeding equipment changes from a standalone RM1,000 relief to a sub-item of the RM10,000 health cap. For someone who already hits RM10,000 on health, this is a small effective reduction.
- For a typical salaried employee the SOCSO/EIS relief is RM350 as before. The extra RM150 applies only to voluntary LINDUNG 24 or non-mandatory LINDUNG KENDIRI contributions.
- Treat spouse, child, EPF + life insurance, childcare and disabled reliefs as unchanged from YA2026, because they were not in the tax measures. Confirm against the Finance Bill when it is published.

### Gaps
- YYC Advisors says the old RM2,000 upskilling relief "runs until YA2026" and that breastfeeding equipment is "claimable yearly". The MOF appendix text I extracted states neither, so both are unverified (single secondary source).
- The "17 sektor bukan mandatori" for LINDUNG KENDIRI are not listed in the appendix.

## Q3. Rebates: RM400 self/spouse, RM35,000 threshold, zakat, departure levy

### Takeaway
No change was announced. None of the 36 tax-measure appendices concerns personal rebates, the RM35,000 threshold, zakat/fitrah rebates or the departure levy rebate. The only zakat item is non-tax: EPF Simpanan Shariah members may pay zakat directly from dividends.

### Cited Findings
- Appendix titles 1–36 cover individual rates (1), reliefs (2–9), TSUWAS school funds (10), SME rates (11), and business, stamp duty, sales tax, service tax and entertainment duty items (12–36). None is on rebates or departure levy — [MOF Langkah Cukai, Senarai Lampiran](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/langkah-cukai.pdf)
- The only "rebat" items in the speech are non-income-tax: NUR MADANI appliance rebates up to RM200, and rebates to organisers of large events (para 179) — [MOF Ucapan](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf)
- Speech: "Kini, ahli KWSP yang memilih Simpanan Shariah juga boleh membayar zakat terus daripada dividen, seiring kemudahan di PNB dan Lembaga Tabung Haji." — same source
- MissLobang's worked examples still assume "the RM400 rebate that applies when chargeable income is RM35,000 or less" and do not report any change — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.my/article/budget-2027-income-tax-changes-malaysia)

### Inferences
- Model YA2027 with the RM400 self rebate, the RM400 spouse rebate and the RM35,000 threshold unchanged. The higher RM12,000 relief will pull some taxpayers under RM35,000 chargeable income, so more people get the rebate even though the rule is unchanged.

### Gaps
- No LHDN statement explicitly confirming "rebates unchanged" was found. This finding rests on the absence of a measure in MOF documents.

## Q4. PCB/MTD, EPF rates, SOCSO/EIS, minimum wage, EPF wage floor

### Takeaway
Minimum wage rises from RM1,700 to RM2,000 from June 2027 (micro, small and medium enterprises with sales under RM50m are exempt), and a RM2,500 starting minimum is proposed for semi-skilled workers and graduates. No change to EPF contribution rates, the EPF wage floor, or SOCSO/EIS contribution rates was announced. No PCB/MTD rule change was announced. LHDN's 2027 PCB schedule had not been found as of early November.

### Cited Findings
- **Speech para 32 (verbatim):** "Oleh itu, Kerajaan bersetuju untuk meningkatkan kadar gaji minimum daripada 1,700 kepada 2,000 ringgit. Kadar ini berkuat kuasa mulai Jun 2027. PMKS dengan hasil jualan di bawah 50 juta ringgit dikecualikan bagi memberi ruang untuk penyesuaian model perniagaan. Ini bermakna, lebih 4 juta pekerja tertakluk kepada kadar gaji minimum baharu." — [MOF Ucapan](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf). Same in [The Star, 9 Oct 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/10/09/budget-2027-minimum-wage-to-rise-to-rm2000-from-june-2027-says-anwar)
- The speech also introduces "gaji minimum bagi pekerjaan separa mahir dan graduan pada 2,500 ringgit sebulan sebagai permulaan", and welcomes GLIC/GLC raising the living-wage benchmark to RM3,400 from RM3,100 — [MOF Ucapan](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf)
- **EPF:** automatic EPF membership at age 18 (para 35); EPF matching contributions for e-hailing and p-hailing drivers of up to RM600/yr or RM6,000 lifetime; i-Emas monthly withdrawals at ages 55 and 60 (para 76); members under 55 may pay MediAsas premiums from Akaun Sejahtera (para 86). No statutory contribution-rate change appears — same source; [activpayroll, 2 Nov 2026](https://www.activpayroll.com/news-articles/malaysia-budget-2027-proposed-payroll-tax-and-employment-changes) ("No changes to contribution rates or wage floors are mentioned")
- **SOCSO/EIS:** the speech has 35% PERKESO matching contributions for e-hailing and p-hailing workers (50% if platforms contribute), and Tabung Haji paying PERKESO contributions for 40,000 mosque officials. No change to employee SOCSO/EIS rates or wage ceiling was found — [MOF Ucapan](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf)
- **PCB:** activpayroll notes the rate changes "would need to be reflected in PCB calculations" if confirmed, with no other PCB change — [activpayroll](https://www.activpayroll.com/news-articles/malaysia-budget-2027-proposed-payroll-tax-and-employment-changes). MissLobang says LHDN had not published the 2027 PCB schedule as of its writing (~17 Oct 2026) — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.my/article/budget-2027-income-tax-changes-malaysia)
- Employer-side items affecting payroll: tax deduction on salary only if paid via financial institution or cashless instrument (SMEs excepted), from YA2028 (Lampiran 14); employer deduction for PTPTN repayments made for employees (Lampiran 30) — [MOF Langkah Cukai](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/langkah-cukai.pdf). activpayroll gives the PTPTN window as payments 1 Jan 2027 – 31 Dec 2028 (secondary; appendix body not reviewed) — [activpayroll](https://www.activpayroll.com/news-articles/malaysia-budget-2027-proposed-payroll-tax-and-employment-changes)

### Inferences
- The minimum-wage rise is mid-year (June 2027). It is not a tax measure, but it affects gross pay and therefore EPF, SOCSO and EIS amounts for minimum-wage workers from June 2027.
- Expect LHDN to issue a revised PCB computation (the Schedule/formula under the Income Tax (Deduction from Remuneration) Rules) reflecting the new bands and RM12,000 relief for 2027 payrolls, once the law is in place. Not confirmed.

### Gaps
- The legal instrument for the RM2,000 minimum wage (Minimum Wages Order) and the exact June 2027 date are not yet found.
- No LHDN PCB 2027 specification or calculator update was found.

## Q5. Effective year of assessment, backdating, phasing

### Takeaway
Every individual measure is stated as "Mulai tahun taksiran 2027" (from YA2027), except tourism relief, which is "Bagi tahun taksiran 2027" (YA2027 only). Nothing is backdated to YA2026 and nothing is phased.

### Cited Findings
- Lampiran 1 (rates), 2 (personal relief), 3 (parents), 4 (health), 5 (education), 6 (lifestyle), 7 (sports) and 9 (SOCSO): "Tarikh Kuat Kuasa: Mulai tahun taksiran 2027" — [MOF Langkah Cukai](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/langkah-cukai.pdf)
- Lampiran 8 (tourism): "Bagi tahun taksiran 2027" — same source
- MissLobang: "Everything starts from the year of assessment 2027", meaning income earned 1 Jan – 31 Dec 2027, filed in 2028 — [MissLobang](https://www.misslobang.my/article/budget-2027-income-tax-changes-malaysia)
- Related housing item (not income tax): first-home stamp duty exemption is widened to homes up to RM750,000 (100% on the first RM500,000, 50% on the balance) for SPAs executed 1 Jan 2027 – 31 Dec 2030 — [MOF Langkah Cukai, Lampiran 16](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/langkah-cukai.pdf)

### Inferences
- YA2026 (income year 2026, filed in 2027) keeps the old table and RM9,000 relief. A calculator needs both tables selected by YA.

### Gaps
- None on effective dates. Enactment is still pending (Q6).

## Q6. Official MOF documents, figures, and differences vs media; enacted vs proposed

### Takeaway
MOF published the Malay speech text (with the tax measures reproduced as Lampiran II), a standalone "Langkah Cukai" PDF, a summary (touchpoint) PDF, and Fiscal/Economic Outlook web pages. The two official documents agree with each other and with the main media on every figure. The only media differences are loose band wording ("RM70,000 to RM100,000"). Legal status as of 6 Nov 2026: only the Supply Bill 2027 (spending) has passed its policy stage. No Finance Bill carrying the tax changes was found tabled or passed.

### Cited Findings
- Budget portal documents: Ucapan Belanjawan (ub27.pdf), Ringkasan Belanjawan (touchpoint-budget-bm.pdf), Langkah Cukai (langkah-cukai.pdf), and web pages for Tinjauan Fiskal, Tinjauan Ekonomi and Anggaran Perbelanjaan — [belanjawan.mof.gov.my](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/)
- Speech para 23 on the appendices: "Selebihnya teks penuh dan lampiran adalah sahih sepertimana edaran" (the rest of the full text and appendices are authentic as circulated). The PM read a shortened version — [MOF Ucapan](https://belanjawan.mof.gov.my/pdf/belanjawan2027/ucapan/ub27.pdf)

### Inferences
- The YA2027 figures should be stored as "proposed (Budget 2027, 9 Oct 2026)" until a Finance Act 2026 (or similarly named act) amending Schedule 1 and section 46 of the Income Tax Act 1967 is gazetted.
