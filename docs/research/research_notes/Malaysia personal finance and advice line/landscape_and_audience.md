Scope: Malaysia, as of 2026-11-06. All URLs accessed 2026-11-06. Tags: **[fetched]** = I read the page itself. **[snippet]** = taken from search-result summaries, not read in full (moderate confidence). **(LC)** = single source or weak source (lower confidence). ## 1. Who serves Malaysians' personal finance today: what each does, how it makes money, and whether it is neutral

### Takeaway
The market is split into separate pieces. Government and statutory bodies (EPF, AKPK, BNM/FEN, LHDN) are neutral and free, but each covers only its own area (retirement, debt, tax). Commercial players either sell or refer products (comparison sites, robo-advisers, banks, e-wallets) or use calculators to bring in leads for payroll software or investment products. Budgeting apps are mostly foreign freemium apps with manual entry, because Malaysian bank-feed integration is limited. No neutral tool takes one person from gross salary through deductions and commitments to wealth projection.

### Cited Findings

**Government and statutory tools (free, neutral, siloed)**
- **EPF/KWSP i-Akaun app: Retirement Goal Calculator.** It takes salary, expected salary growth, i-Simpan contributions, expected dividend and retirement age, then compares the projection with the RIA tiers. Voluntary top-ups go through i-Simpan and i-Topup. [snippet] — [RinggitPlus retirement guide, updated 22 Sep 2026](https://ringgitplus.com/en/guides/find-out-how-much-you-need-to-retire-with-this-calculator/)
- **EPF Retirement Income Adequacy (RIA) Framework**, launched 12 Dec 2024 and in force from 1 Jan 2026. It has three tiers at age 60: Basic RM390,000, Adequate RM650,000 and Enhanced RM1.3 million. Adequate is 240 × RM2,690/month. It replaced the single RM240,000 target used from 2008 to 2025. Sources disagree on how fast Basic is phased in (one says RM270k in 2026 rising by RM30k a year to 2030, another says RM290k in 2026, RM340k in 2027 and RM390k in 2028). [snippet] — [The Star, 12 Dec 2024](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2024/12/12/epf-launches-three-tier-savings-framework-for-different-retirement-lifestyles); [SAYS](https://says.com/my/lifestyle/makecents/epf-rolls-out-major-changes-including-tighter-withdrawal-rules-and-new-retirement-benchmarks-for-2026); [RinggitPlus](https://ringgitplus.com/en/guides/find-out-how-much-you-need-to-retire-with-this-calculator/)
- **KWSP Belanjawanku 2024/2025** is an expenditure guide by EPF and UM's Social Wellbeing Research Centre, released 12 Dec 2024. It gives minimum monthly budgets for a "reasonable standard of living" in 12 cities. Examples: a single retiree in the Klang Valley needs RM2,690; a single public-transport user in Alor Setar needs RM1,600. It is free as a PDF and as the Belanjawanku app (iOS/Android/Huawei). Earlier editions came out in 2019 (Klang Valley only) and 2022/2023 (with an expense-tracking app). I found no newer edition than 2024/2025. [snippet] — [The Star, 12 Dec 2024](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2024/12/12/retirees-need-rm2690-a-month-for-reasonable-standard-of-living); [Malay Mail, 13 Dec 2024](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2024/12/13/how-much-to-spend-monthly-and-save-for-later-day-your-guide-to-understanding-epfs-new-belanjawanku-and-retirement-framework/159667); [The Star, 13 Jun 2023](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2023/06/13/epf-launches-expenditure-guide-for-households-app-to-track-expenses-online)
- **AKPK** (set up by BNM) offers free counselling, a Debt Management Programme (DMP) and financial education, with hotline 1800 88 2575. Its **POWER! (Pengurusan Wang Ringgit Anda)** programme is "specially designed to equip young individuals with essential financial knowledge" and has an online learning portal at power.akpk.org.my. BNM's page also lists an Android app, "AKPK-Financial Service for Mobile". [fetched] — [BNM Financial Capability and Empowerment page](https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-capability-and-empowerment). POWER! handbooks were historically sold for RM5. [snippet] — [BNM POWER! press release (undated)](https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/pengurusan-wang-ringgit-anda-power-programme)
- **AKPK "Financial Insight" app** (with a card network, around 2017) calculated financial health from user inputs and projected retirement standing. I could not confirm it is still live. (LC) [snippet] — [iMoney](https://www.imoney.my/articles/akpk-visa-launches-retirement-planning-app)
- **AKPK DMP**: more than 113,000 individuals and businesses helped in 2023–2024, and about 495,000 borrowers assisted cumulatively as of Dec 2024 (MOF). [snippet] — [NST, Apr 2025](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/news/nation/2025/04/1201599/akpk-helped-113000-escape-debt); [Newswav/MOF, Mar 2025](https://newswav.com/article/akpk-benefits-1-4-mln-individuals-as-of-december-2024-finance-ministry-A2503_QMnt8c)
- **BNM / Financial Education Network (FEN)**: FEN is an inter-agency body set up in Nov 2016 and co-chaired by BNM and the SC. Members include MOE, MOHE, EPF, PIDM, PNB and AKPK. It runs fenetwork.my. The **National Strategy for Financial Literacy 2026–2030 (NS2.0)** launched on 3 Oct 2025 with five strategic priorities, and a National Coordination Office hosted at BNM opens Jan 2026. Financial Literacy Month 2025's "Skuad Celik Kewangan" roadshow visited 111 locations. The NS2.0 press release names retirement planners, households facing shocks, vulnerable groups, people with disabilities and rural micro-entrepreneurs as target groups. **It does not mention young adults or first-jobbers.** [fetched] — [BNM NS2.0 press release](https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/flmns2pr)
- **BNM's other consumer channels**: BNMLINK/BNMTELELINK (1-300-88-5465, telelink.bnm.gov.my), MobileLINK, the Ombudsman for Financial Services, and eABM Connect. All of these handle complaints and enquiries; none is a planning tool. [fetched] — [BNM Financial Capability page](https://www.bnm.gov.my/financial-capability-and-empowerment)

You enter monthly income, EPF/SOCSO, dependants (TP1) and reliefs or zakat. Its flows are built around employers and payroll, and guides stress that PCB is only an estimate of final tax. ### Inferences
- The neutral options (EPF, AKPK, FEN, LHDN, Belanjawanku) are each limited to one agency's mandate. EPF covers retirement and its own savings; LHDN covers tax and is employer-oriented; AKPK covers debt and remedial help. None combines take-home pay, commitments and projections.
- Almost every free salary calculator is lead generation for payroll SaaS, an investment platform or a recruiter. A neutral salary breakdown is a plausible way to stand out, especially one that explains each line rather than just showing totals.
- An education-only app would fall outside the SC's finfluencer and investment-advice rules only if it avoids product recommendations. This is worth confirming in a separate legal-scope note.

## 2. What these tools miss for someone who wants to see their whole financial picture without linking accounts

### Takeaway
A first-jobber has to piece their picture together from at least five places: a payroll-vendor calculator or LHDN for take-home pay, EPF i-Akaun for retirement, Belanjawanku for spending benchmarks, a bank app for spending (one bank only), and a comparison site or credit bureau for credit and debt. Each uses its own terms and assumptions, and the commercial ones end in a product pitch. No neutral, account-free tool connects these and explains the numbers.

### Cited Findings
- Bank apps only track their own transactions (each covers only that bank's accounts, cards and QR payments). [fetched] — [SmartCalc MY guide](https://smartcalc-my.vercel.app/guides/best-budgeting-apps-malaysia); [RinggitPlus on MAE](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/e-wallet/maybank-launches-new-mae-by-maybank2u-app-offers-new-features-for-better-financial-management.html)
- Bank-feed aggregation for Malaysian banks is limited in 2026, so third-party budgeting apps rely on manual or CSV entry. (LC) [fetched] — [SmartCalc MY guide](https://smartcalc-my.vercel.app/guides/best-budgeting-apps-malaysia)
- The EPF Retirement Goal Calculator lives inside the logged-in i-Akaun app and projects EPF savings against RIA tiers. It does not cover take-home pay, debt or non-EPF savings. [snippet] — [RinggitPlus retirement guide](https://ringgitplus.com/en/guides/find-out-how-much-you-need-to-retire-with-this-calculator/)
- Retirement benchmarks changed recently: RM240k at 55 was used from 2008 to 2025, and the RIA tiers at 60 apply from 2026. Sources disagree on the Basic phase-in schedule. Different figures (36%, 38.3%, 41.2%, 23.9%) are reported for "members meeting Basic Savings" depending on denominator and benchmark. This confuses lay readers. [snippet] — [Business Today, 8 Oct 2025](https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2025/10/08/only-one-quarter-of-epf-members-meet-basic-retirement-savings-says-mof/); [The Vibes](https://www.thevibes.com/articles/news/113744/majority-of-epf-members-fall-short-of-basic-retirement-savings-mof-acknowledges); [SAYS](https://says.com/my/lifestyle/makecents/epf-rolls-out-major-changes-including-tighter-withdrawal-rules-and-new-retirement-benchmarks-for-2026)
- The PCB calculator is built for payroll cases (e.g. "Previously NOT Employed in the Current Year"), and PCB is only an estimate, with the final tax settled at filing. Unofficial calculators often use simplified formulas or old (YA 2024) rates. [snippet] — [BrioHR](https://support.briohr.com/knowledge/how-to-calculate-pcb-using-lhdn-calculator); [sorted-my](https://hlteoh37.github.io/sorted-my/pcb-calculator.html); [SmartCalc MY](https://smartcalc-my.vercel.app/income-tax-calculator-malaysia)
- BNPL is outside traditional household-debt metrics, which count housing loans, credit cards and personal loans. A user's own "commitments" view would likewise miss BNPL unless it is entered explicitly. [snippet] — [The Vibes opinion](https://www.thevibes.com/articles/opinion/117507/buy-now-pay-later-schemes-pose-emerging-financial-risks-for-young-malaysians)
- 68% of Malaysians use social media as their main source of financial learning (RMFLS 2025). Trust in it rose (score 4.3, up from 4.0), and new SC rules target finfluencers. [fetched] — [RinggitPlus RMFLS 2025](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/featured/rmfls-2025-middle-income-malaysians-hit-by-financial-squeeze-as-youth-build-stronger-habits.html)
- 26% of Malaysians don't know what a credit score is (RMFLS 2025, down from 30% in 2024). [fetched] — [RinggitPlus RMFLS 2025](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/featured/rmfls-2025-middle-income-malaysians-hit-by-financial-squeeze-as-youth-build-stronger-habits.html)
- The BNM Deputy Governor said knowledge is improving but "behaviours are not shifting" (2 Oct 2025). The Monash/Versa/AHAM study (Jun 2026) calls this a "knowledge-action gap". [fetched] — [BNM speech](https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/dgaz-nfls4); [The Star, 10 Jun 2026](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2026/06/10/study-finds-gap-between-financial-literacy-and-financial-security-among-malaysians)

### Inferences
- The gap is not a lack of information. It is the lack of a single, neutral, account-free view that runs gross pay → statutory deductions → net pay → commitments/DSR → savings rate → EPF and wealth projection, using the official benchmarks (RIA tiers, Belanjawanku) as reference points instead of product pitches.
- Because Malaysian bank aggregation is limited, manual entry is already the norm. An app that needs no linking is not at a disadvantage against local competitors.
- Showing which benchmark version applies (RIA 2026 phase-in vs the old RM240k) and stating assumptions next to figures would directly address a confusion visible in the press.

### Gaps
- I found no user research (reviews, forum threads, Lowyat or Reddit) specifically documenting first-jobbers' frustration with scattered tools. The gap is inferred from feature coverage, not from voice-of-customer data.
- I did not confirm whether EPF's calculator is reachable without login on the KWSP website.

## 3. What the data says about Malaysians' financial literacy, savings, debt, EPF adequacy and young workers' habits

### Takeaway
Literacy scores are flat to slightly rising (MYFLIC 57.1 in 2018, 59.0 in 2021, 59.1 in 2024; knowledge 60.2% in 2021 to 62.9% in 2024), but behaviour lags. 61% struggle to raise RM1,000 in an emergency, only 58% actively save, about 1 in 4 find their debt burdensome, and only about 36–41% of active EPF members meet the age-based Basic Savings level, with just 18.6% of 18–30-year-olds on track (Aug 2025). Youth bankruptcies are rising (877 in 2024) and BNPL is growing fast. Young earners start from low pay: the formal-sector median for ages 20–24 was about RM2,045 in Mar 2025.

## 4. Malaysian products offering a "financial health score" or similar, and how they are received

### Takeaway
Several have existed: a comparison site's financial health check, a WhatsApp bot (2020), another comparison site's credit score (2018), AKPK's Financial Insight app (~2017), a financial-advisory firm's app and a credit bureau's score app. Most are either credit scores (from credit-bureau data) or questionnaires run by product-selling or advisory firms. I found almost no published evidence of how users received them (reviews, adoption numbers). The one active neutral-looking example, a comparison site's quiz, sits inside a referral business.

### Cited Findings
- **A comparison site's financial health check**: built with a financial-wellness startup. It is a free online questionnaire covering 7 areas (including cash flow, debt management, estate planning and insurance) and gives an overall score plus flagged areas and tips. Scoring was designed by the site's licensed financial planners, and data is handled under the PDPA. It was still promoted as a "Financial Health Quiz" in late-2025 CIMB Foundation workshops. Launch date not found. [snippet] — [RinggitPlus launch post](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/ringgitplus/ringgitplus-launches-free-financial-health-check-tool-for-all-malaysians.html); [Newswav, Dec 2025](https://newswav.com/article/ringgitplus-and-cimb-foundation-continue-financial-literacy-mission-with-go-A2512_i9cdXl)
- **The same site's quiz** (RMFLS 2025) compares you with other Malaysians. [snippet] — [RinggitPlus Money Meter](https://ringgitplus.com/en/ringgitplus-malaysian-financial-literacy-survey-2025/rmfls-money-meter/)
- **myRinggitHealth** (2020) was a WhatsApp chatbot that checked your credit facilities. Current status unknown. (LC) [snippet] — [Newswav (2020)](https://newswav.com/article/A2009_R6Mcu7)

### Inferences
- In Malaysia, "financial health score" most often means a credit score (CCRIS or a private credit bureau) or a lead-generating questionnaire. A transparent, formula-based indicator that shows its inputs and thresholds (savings rate, DSR, emergency months, EPF vs RIA tier), with no product follow-up, would be unusual.
- Many of these tools are from 2017–2020 and have little visible follow-up coverage, which suggests they faded. This is not confirmed.

### Gaps
- I found no reviews, ratings, adoption or retention figures for any Malaysian financial-health tool. Reception is unknown. App-store review mining would be needed.
- I could not confirm which of the older score tools above, including AKPK Financial Insight, are still live in Nov 2026.
- I found no Malaysian bank offering an in-app "financial health score" in 2025–26. I did not search exhaustively.
