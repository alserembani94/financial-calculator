# Petawang

A personal finance discovery web app that helps people discover their own financial picture from public, scattered information, starting with their salary. It educates and compares; it doesn't advise or sell.

## Language

### Salary

**Gross salary**:
A person's regular monthly salary before deductions, without allowances, overtime or bonus.
_Avoid_: basic pay, income, wage

**Breakdown**:
The split of a gross salary into the employee's statutory deductions (EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB), take-home, and the employer's contributions.
_Avoid_: payslip, summary

**Take-home**:
Gross salary less the employee's EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB for the month.
_Avoid_: net pay, net income, bring-home

**Financial year**:
The calendar year whose statutory rules a breakdown uses.
_Avoid_: tax year, assessment year

### Insights

**Insight**:
A value computed from the user's own inputs, sometimes projected over time, and compared with a published benchmark.
_Avoid_: advice, recommendation, tip, score

**Signal**:
A short label saying where one measure sits against its benchmark, such as "Above BNM's 60% high-leverage marker". There is one signal per measure, never one for the whole person.
_Avoid_: rating, score, grade, verdict, health score

**Projection**:
An estimate of a value at a future date, worked out from the user's inputs and stated assumptions.
_Avoid_: forecast, prediction, target

**Benchmark**:
A figure published by a named public institution, with its source and date, that an insight is compared with.
_Avoid_: standard, rule of thumb, target

**Commitment**:
A regular repayment the user owes, such as a loan or an instalment plan.
_Avoid_: debt, expense, liability
